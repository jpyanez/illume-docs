# Policies and help

## Contact

- **Slack channel #illume-users** is the main way to reach the admins and other users:
    - outages and recoveries are posted there automatically;
    - announcements of maintenance and changes;
    - report problems and ask questions there.
- **How to join #illume-users:** send a private Slack message to **juanpablo** on the IceCube or P-ONE Slack workspace, asking to be added.
- **Accounts and private matters** (new account, new key, leaving the group): email the cluster owner, Juan Pablo Yáñez, at [j.p.yanez@ualberta.ca](mailto:j.p.yanez@ualberta.ca).

## Fair use

- illume is shared. HTCondor balances use between users ("fair share"): if you have used a lot recently, other users' jobs start first.
- Request the resources your jobs really need ([details](jobs.md#ask-for-what-you-need-not-more)).
- Don't run heavy computations on the login node.
- Don't share your account or your private key.
- Use illume for research only. No cryptocurrency mining, no unrelated services.

## Data

- **There are no backups.** You are responsible for keeping copies of your important data elsewhere.
- Accounts and data are deleted **6 months after you leave the group** ([details](access.md#leaving-the-group)).

## Maintenance

The cluster may be taken down for maintenance or hardware work. Planned downtime is announced in #illume-users in advance whenever possible.

## Acknowledging illume

illume was funded by the Canada Foundation for Innovation (CFI) and Alberta Innovates. If you use illume for work you publish or present, please include an acknowledgement such as:

> This research was enabled in part by the illume computing cluster at the University of Alberta, funded by the Canada Foundation for Innovation and Alberta Innovates.
